# UniStay – Student Services Platform

UniStay is a comprehensive **Student Services Platform** designed to provide students with convenient access to essential services through a centralized digital platform.

The platform connects **SLIIT students, non-SLIIT students, and service vendors** within a single system. It provides a range of student-oriented services including **accommodation, food, laundry, cleaning, and maintenance services**.

Non-SLIIT students and vendors can access the platform through an **annual subscription model**, while SLIIT students can use the platform to access the available services.

---

## 1. Project Overview

UniStay is a full-stack web application developed to simplify the process of accessing, requesting, and managing essential student services.

The platform provides a centralized environment where students can access different services while vendors can provide and manage their respective services. Administrators can manage users, subscriptions, service-related activities, and customer support requests through the system.

### User Types

| User Type              | Description                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **SLIIT Students**     | Access and use services available through the platform.                  |
| **Non-SLIIT Students** | Access the platform through an annual subscription.                      |
| **Vendors**            | Provide services to students through an annual vendor subscription.      |
| **Administrators**     | Manage users, services, subscriptions, requests, and support activities. |

### Services

UniStay provides the following major services:

* **Accommodation Management**
* **Food Supply Management**
* **Laundry Management**
* **Cleaning & Maintenance Management**
* **Customer Support and Ticketing**

The platform brings multiple student services together in one centralized environment, reducing the need to manage different services through separate platforms.

## 2. Key Features

### User Management

The User Management module handles the registration and administration of users across the platform.

* Student registration and account management
* SLIIT and non-SLIIT student management
* Vendor registration and management
* User profile management
* Role-based access control
* User status management
* User information management
* Subscription-related user management

### Ticketing and Support System

The Ticketing System provides a structured communication channel between users and administrators for handling support requests and issues.

* Create and submit support tickets
* Ticket categorization
* Ticket priority management
* Ticket assignment
* Ticket status tracking
* Administrator responses
* Ticket history management
* Ticket resolution and closure

### Subscription Management

UniStay follows an annual subscription model for non-SLIIT students and service vendors.

* Annual student subscription management
* Annual vendor subscription management
* Subscription status tracking
* Subscription validity management
* Subscription-related account management

### Food Supply Management

The Food Supply Management module manages food-related services provided through the platform.

* Food service management
* Food vendor management
* Food item management
* Food availability management
* Food supply tracking
* Order-related management

### Accommodation Management

The Accommodation Management module manages accommodation-related services and information.

* Accommodation listing management
* Property and room information
* Availability management
* Accommodation provider management
* Student accommodation requests
* Accommodation information management

### Laundry Management

The Laundry Management module supports the management of laundry services and requests.

* Laundry service management
* Laundry request management
* Request status tracking
* Service provider management
* Laundry request progress tracking
* Service completion management

### Cleaning & Maintenance Management

The Cleaning & Maintenance Management module handles cleaning services and maintenance-related requests through a centralized system.

* Cleaning service management
* Maintenance request management
* Service provider management
* Service availability management
* Request categorization
* Request status tracking
* Service progress tracking
* Request completion management
